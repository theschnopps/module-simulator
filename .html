Method: each purchase has a 1/X chance of yielding a rare item, but if that many purchases pass without one, the pity counter forces a guaranteed rare on that purchase (resetting the counter either way). Rare types start with an equal share of that rare pool (adjusted for any that are already maxed, based on what you've entered). Once a type reaches its target copy count, its share drops by the reduction amount and the freed percentage is spread evenly across the remaining not-yet-maxed types — mirroring "soft pity" systems where completed items keep dropping as duplicates instead of disappearing. Because both the pity timing and the redistribution path are random, there's no closed-form formula — this tool re-runs the process many times to estimate the true average.
